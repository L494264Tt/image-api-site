# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our Pledge / 我们的承诺

We want this project to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming space for contributors and users.

我们希望本项目对贡献者和用户保持尊重、务实和友好。

## Expected Behavior / 期望行为

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept feedback when impact differs from intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Share enough context for others to understand and review your work.

- 保持尊重和建设性。
- 可以默认善意，但当实际影响不同于初衷时，应接受反馈。
- 讨论聚焦项目和用户。
- 提供足够上下文，方便他人理解和审查改动。

## Unacceptable Behavior / 不可接受行为

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Security disclosure in public before maintainers have a reasonable chance to respond.

- 骚扰、侮辱、威胁或歧视性语言。
- 未经允许公开他人隐私信息。
- 反复进行无关干扰。
- 在维护者有合理响应机会前公开披露安全问题。

## Enforcement / 执行

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.

维护者可以编辑、隐藏或移除违反本准则的评论、issue、PR 或贡献。重复或严重违规可能导致被临时或永久禁止参与项目空间。

Report conduct concerns through the repository owner profile. For security issues, follow `SECURITY.md`.

行为问题请通过仓库所有者主页联系维护者。安全问题请遵循 `SECURITY.md`。
